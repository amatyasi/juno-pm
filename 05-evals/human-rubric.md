## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Customer call scanner
- **Value proposition:** Assess an idea's strengths by collecting customer evidence signals.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence grounding | Is every claim tied to a real customer verbatim and a specific ticket or call, with nothing invented? | Yes / No | No source or not existing | n/a | Every claim has a real verbatim and user name and a ticket/call URL |
| CRM validation | Account status (and ARR) is matching with the CRM data | Yes / No | Customer doesn't exists, or status or ARR is not matching CRM | n/a | Customer appears in CRM, status (prospect, negotiations, active, churned) is real, ARR matches CRM data |
| Feasibility included | _ | Yes / No | Feasibility is missing, or not covering files/services, architecture fit, dependencies, schema or migration impact, technical risk, and testing | n/a | T shirt size exists with the feasibility factors (files/services, architecture fit, dependencies, schema or migration impact, technical risk, and testing) |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 2 a day
- **Who scores them with you:** me and an other PM
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** Meet once a week and sync on disagreed scores.

3 passes
